## Pricing Strategy

### Student Pricing
| Tier | Price | Includes | Rationale |
|------|-------|----------|-----------|
| Free | ₹0/month | 10 AI queries/day, Socratic Tutor only | Must be genuinely useful — not crippled |
| Pro | ₹199/month | Unlimited queries, all 5 features, shareable reports | Priced below a single coaching class session (₹300–₹500) |
| Annual | ₹1,499/year | Same as Pro | Equivalent to ₹125/month — "one month free" |

**Rationale for ₹199:** 
- PhysicsWallah charges ₹0 for most content, ₹2,000–₹8,000 for annual course packages
- Saarthi is a tool, not a course — monthly micro-subscription is psychologically cheaper
- ₹199 is below the "need to ask parents" threshold for most students with a part-time income or pocket money
- Competes with: one Swiggy order, one movie ticket. Frames well.

### Founder Pricing
| Tier | Price | Includes |
|------|-------|----------|
| Free | ₹0/month | India Stack Navigator (1 check), 0 to GST (1 run) |
| Pro | ₹499/month | All 5 features, unlimited runs, Investor Match (20 results), Pitch Roast (3/month) |
| Annual | ₹3,999/year | Same as Pro | 

**Rationale for ₹499:** "Less than 1 hour of a CA's time" is the frame. A CA charges ₹1,000–₹3,000/hour in Tier 1 cities.

## Sensitivity Analysis

**If conversion rate is 1% (pessimistic) instead of 2%/5%:**
- Month 12 MRR: ~₹71,840 (~$860)

**If user growth is 10% MoM instead of 20% (slower organic growth):**
- Month 12 users: ~6,000 students, ~1,500 founders
- Month 12 MRR: ~₹53,550 (~$640)

**If conversion rate hits 4%/8% (optimistic — product-market fit achieved):**
- Month 12 MRR: ~₹2,87,360 (~$3,450)

**The honest assessment:** Year 1 revenue will likely be ₹0 (first 6 months pre-paywall) to ₹1,50,000. This is a product-building year, not a revenue year.

---

## Flags

**Red Flags:**
- Indian EdTech monetisation is brutally hard. Byju's had millions of users before it found (then lost) its revenue model. Do not assume 2% conversion until you've seen real user behaviour with a paywall.

**Yellow Flags:**
- Founder segment will likely convert at higher rates than student segment but has slower user acquisition. Don't neglect founders because the student funnel is bigger.
- If annual subscriptions are offered from Day 1, cash flow looks better but creates refund obligations.
