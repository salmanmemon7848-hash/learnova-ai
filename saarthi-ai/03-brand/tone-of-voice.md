# Tone of Voice

**Phase:** 5 — Brand  
**Project:** saarthi-ai  
**Date:** 2026-05-22

---

## Brand Personality Traits

| Trait | What it means for Saarthi |
|-------|--------------------------|
| **Sharp** | Direct, no filler. Gets to the point. The student has 3 hours of study left — don't waste 30 seconds with pleasantries. |
| **Warm** | Not clinical. Understands the pressure Indian students and founders are under. Like a brilliant senior friend, not a textbook. |
| **Honest** | Tells you what you need to hear. Won't call your wrong answer "interesting." Won't say your pitch is "promising" when it has a fatal flaw. |
| **Indian** | Uses natural Indian English cadence. Comfortable with "Hinglish" when it fits. References Indian context without forcing it. |

---

## Voice Principles

**We are:** The brilliant, honest senior who tells you the truth because they actually care.  
**We are not:** The motivational poster. The corporate edtech platform. The global AI with an Indian flag sticker.

**We are:** Concise and dense with value.  
**We are not:** Padded with "Great question!" and "Certainly!" and "As an AI language model..."

**We are:** Specific to India — NCERT, JEE, RBI, GST, PW, Kota.  
**We are not:** Generic global — "study materials," "regulatory compliance," "educational content."

## Writing Samples by Context

### Homepage Headline
**Good:** "The AI that teaches you to think — not just to copy answers. Built for India's hardest exams."  
**Bad:** "Revolutionize your learning journey with AI-powered personalized education."

### Error Message (AI query limit reached)
**Good:** "You've used your 10 free queries for today. Come back tomorrow — or go Pro for ₹199/month and keep going."  
**Bad:** "You have reached your daily usage limit. Please consider upgrading to our premium subscription plan."

### Social Media Post (about Socratic Tutor)
**Good:** "Every time you ask Saarthi a physics question, it asks you one back. We refuse to cheat you out of the understanding."  
**Bad:** "Saarthi's innovative Socratic learning methodology leverages advanced AI to enhance critical thinking skills."

### Customer Welcome Email
**Good:** "You signed up. Good. Here's how Saarthi works: ask it a doubt, and it won't just give you the answer — it'll ask you 3 questions until you find it yourself. This is annoying at first. Then it stops being annoying. Then you start getting the right answers on your own. That's the whole point."  
**Bad:** "Welcome to Saarthi! We are thrilled to have you on board. Our platform offers a comprehensive suite of AI-powered learning tools..."

### Pitch Roast feedback (Founder section)
**Good:** "Your TAM slide says ₹5 lakh crore. That's the entire FMCG market. Your actual addressable market for a B2B inventory tool for Kirana stores is probably 2% of that — ₹10,000 crore. That's still worth pursuing. Use the real number."  
**Bad:** "Great work on your pitch! You might want to consider refining your market size analysis for clarity."

## Vocabulary Guide

| Use | Avoid |
|-----|-------|
| "doubt" (student's unclear concept) | "confusion" (too generic) |
| "PYQ" (previous year questions) | "historical questions" |
| "mock" (practice test) | "simulation assessment" |
| "DPIIT registration" | "startup registration" (too vague) |
| "Tier 2/3 city" | "smaller city" (condescending) |
| "crore / lakh" | "$1M" (always use Indian numbering for Indian content) |
| "Hinglish" | — (just use it naturally when it fits) |
| "co-pilot" | "assistant" (co-pilot implies partnership, assistant implies subservience) |
| "we" | "I" for Saarthi as a product voice |

## Flags

**Red Flags:**
- None

**Yellow Flags:**
- "Honest" tone can become harsh. Always pair honest feedback with a clear next step. "Your pitch TAM is wrong AND here's how to fix it" — not just "your pitch TAM is wrong."
