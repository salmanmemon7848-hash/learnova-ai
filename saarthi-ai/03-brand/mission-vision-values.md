## Brand Name

**Saarthi** (सारथी)

**Meaning:** Charioteer, guide. In the Mahabharata, Krishna was Arjuna's Saarthi — not a warrior himself, but the one who guided the warrior to perform at his highest potential. The metaphor is perfect:
- For students: the AI is your guide before the exam battle (JEE/NEET is genuinely war for Indian students)
- For founders: the AI guides you through the startup journey, it doesn't build the startup for you
- It's distinctly Indian, historically rich, and pronounceable in every Indian language

**Domain options (in order of preference):**
1. `getsaarthi.com`
2. `saarthi.ai`
3. `saarthiai.in`
4. `trysaarthi.in`

## Values

### 1. Think Before You Answer
We build AI that develops understanding, not dependence. Saarthi asks questions before giving answers. We never optimise for "giving the user what they want" if what they want is a shortcut past the learning.

*In practice:* Socratic Tutor is the product embodiment of this value. It's also why we don't build a "ChatGPT clone."

### 2. India First, Always
Every feature is designed for Indian users — Indian exam patterns, Indian pricing psychology, Indian regulatory reality, Indian mobile hardware constraints. We don't port global tools into Indian packaging. We build from the ground up for how Indians study and build businesses.

*In practice:* Bharat Pricing Engine, India Stack Navigator, PYQ Predictor — all India-first, not adapted.

### 3. Lightweight by Design
We treat every token as if it costs money — because soon it will. Every feature is the leanest version that solves the problem. No bloat. No "AI for the sake of AI." This isn't a constraint — it's a philosophy that keeps us honest.

*In practice:* Groq key rotation, response caching, DB-first logic, small model selection.

### 4. Honest Over Comfortable
We don't tell students their wrong answer was "almost right" when it wasn't. We don't tell founders their pitch is "good" when it isn't. Hard feedback with respect is more valuable than comfortable validation.

*In practice:* Pitch Roast feature, Mistake DNA that shows error rates clearly, Socratic method that makes you confront your knowledge gap.

### 5. Free Until You're Ready to Pay
The best version of Saarthi should be free enough that a student in a ₹1,200/month hostel in Kota gets real value. Monetisation is for power users who've already decided Saarthi is worth it.

*In practice:* Free tier is generous by design (10 AI queries/day, not 3).
