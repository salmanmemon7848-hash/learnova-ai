## Market Maturity

- **Exam prep:** Growing → maturing. High competition, but AI-native tools are still emerging (2024–2026 is the window)
- **Founder tools:** Early. The Indian founder tooling ecosystem is fragmented and underbuilt relative to market size
- **AI in India:** Emerging → fast growth. Government's India AI Mission (₹10,372 crore budget) is accelerating adoption

---

## Regulatory Summary

- **EdTech:** No specific AI regulation yet. DPDP Act 2023 (India's data protection law) applies — must handle student data carefully. No storing data of minors without parental consent for <13.
- **AI general:** India has draft AI governance framework (2024), non-binding currently. No liability for AI tools yet.
- **Payment:** If monetising later, requires RBI-compliant payment gateway (Razorpay/Cashfree work)
- **Startup tools:** Providing regulatory advice (compliance checklists) is fine as "information," not "legal advice." Standard disclaimer needed.

---

## Timing Assessment

**Why now is the right moment:**
1. Groq's free tier exists and is genuinely powerful enough for production — this window may close as demand grows
2. Indian users are now comfortable with AI interfaces (ChatGPT crossed 10M Indian users in 2023)
3. PhysicsWallah's success proved students will pay for digital ed products
4. India AI Mission funding signals government tailwind for AI EdTech
5. Byju's collapse left ~10M students looking for alternatives — the premium brand is up for grabs

**Timing risks:**
- Google, Meta, OpenAI are all building India-specific products
- Groq may change free tier limits (rate limit reductions have happened before)

---

## Flags

**Red Flags:**
- The market size is real, but conversion from free user to paid is notoriously hard in India. India has a "free first, pay never" culture for digital products. Monetisation timeline must be conservative.

**Yellow Flags:**
- All figures are knowledge-based estimates. Commission independent market research before raising any capital.
- Byju's collapse damaged EdTech investor sentiment. Funding (if ever needed) may be harder.
