## Technology Trends

### 1. Edge AI / Fast Inference is Democratising AI Tooling
Groq's LPU chips deliver 500+ tokens/second — 10x faster than GPT-4 Turbo. This makes real-time AI tutoring (instant doubt clearing) feel responsive even on slow connections. **Strategic implication:** Saarthi can feel faster than well-funded competitors who use slower models.

### 2. Small Models Are Now Surprisingly Capable
LLaMA 3.1 8B (Groq's fastest model) achieves near-GPT-3.5 quality on instruction-following tasks. For structured tasks (MCQ generation, compliance checklists, flashcards), small models perform at 90%+ of large model quality at 10% of the cost. **Strategic implication:** Zero cost + good quality is achievable right now.

### 3. India's Mobile-First Constraint Is a Design Filter
500M+ Indians access the internet only via smartphone. Average phone: Redmi Note (4GB RAM, Android 12). Apps that are heavy, slow, or require high-end hardware are invisible to 80% of the market. **Strategic implication:** PWA-first, <500KB bundle, offline caching is not optional — it's market access.

### 4. AI Tutoring Adoption Is Accelerating Globally
Khan Academy Khanmigo, Duolingo Max, Chegg AI — all launched 2023–2024. India is 12–18 months behind adoption curve. The window to establish a local brand is 2024–2026 before global platforms add India-specific content. **Strategic implication:** Move now.

### 5. Voice AI for Vernacular is Coming
ElevenLabs, Sarvam AI (India-specific), Bhashini (government) are building Hindi/regional TTS and STT. Adding voice input for doubt clearing in Hindi would be a 10x UX improvement for Tier 2/3 students. **Strategic implication:** Design the platform to add voice input as a future layer without rebuilding.

## Behavioural Shifts

- **JEE/NEET aspirants are moving to YouTube + Telegram** away from expensive coaching institutes
- **Founders are increasingly skipping MBA** and learning online — content demand is high
- **India's "free tier" expectation** is softening slightly — UPI and Jio created comfort with micro-payments (₹99–₹299/month feels "like Hotstar" to users)
- **Study at night** is a defining Indian student behaviour — AI availability at 2am is a real advantage over human tutors

## Regulatory Trajectory

- DPDP Act 2023 enforcement will tighten — build privacy-by-design now
- AI governance framework (non-binding draft, 2024) will likely become binding by 2026
- Government's NEP 2020 explicitly promotes technology in education — tailwind for EdTech AI
